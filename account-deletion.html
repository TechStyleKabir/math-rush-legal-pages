Math Rush — Request Account Deletion

Before publishing: deploy this page at a public HTTPS URL and test the request process. The form opens an email draft; it does not send a request automatically. The developer must monitor the support inbox and complete each verified deletion request.

You may request deletion of your Math Rush account and associated cloud score without reinstalling the app.

What will be deleted?

  • The Firebase Authentication account used to sign in to Math Rush.
  • The associated Cloud Firestore users/{uid} document, including display name, best score and update timestamp.

What may remain?

Guest-mode best score, language and audience preferences are stored locally on the device. They can be removed by clearing Math Rush app storage or uninstalling the app. The in-app account-deletion action also clears the local best-score preference. Third-party service logs may be retained under their own policies or where legally required.

Submit a request

Enter the Google account email used for Math Rush. Do not provide a password, verification code, access token, ID token, or other secret.

If the button does not open an email app, send a message manually to Kabir201989@gmail.com with the subject Math Rush account deletion and your Google account email.

The developer will verify the request and delete the matching Firebase Authentication user and Firestore user document. If the requester still has access to the app, the in-app Privacy & Account → Delete account and cloud data flow is available. The developer should not ask for account passwords or authentication tokens.
