Math Rush — Account Deletion

For accounts in app versions that support sign-in, the primary deletion method is the in-app Privacy & Account → Delete account option. Confirm the action in the app to delete the Firebase Authentication account and associated cloud score/profile document.

What deletion covers

  • The Firebase Authentication account used to sign in to Math Rush.
  • The associated Cloud Firestore users/{uid} document used by Math Rush, including its score/profile fields.

Deletion is complete only after the app or developer successfully completes the required deletion operations. If the app displays an error, do not assume the account or cloud data was deleted.

If you cannot access the app

Use the form below to prepare an email request. Submitting the form opens your email application; it does not automatically send the request or delete data. The developer will verify the request and process deletion of the matching account and associated Math Rush cloud document.

If your email application does not open, email Kabir201989@gmail.com with the subject Math Rush account deletion and the Google account email used in the app. Never send a password, verification code, access token or ID token.

What may remain

Guest scores and preferences stored locally on your device are not removed by deleting a cloud account. Clear Math Rush app storage or uninstall the app to remove local data. Google/Firebase and other service providers may retain technical logs under their own policies or where legally required.
