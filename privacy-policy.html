About Math Rush

Math Rush is a timed arithmetic game with scores, streaks, hints, sound effects and English/Hindi interface options. It does not ask players to select an age group or enter a date of birth. Players can use guest play; Google sign-in and cloud score features may be available in builds that include account functionality. The exact data processed depends on the installed app version and the features the player uses.

Information stored on the device

The app stores gameplay preferences and guest-mode scores locally, such as the best score and language preference. Local data remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, except where an in-app deletion action also clears relevant local score data. The app does not need Android permissions for precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files for core gameplay.

Google sign-in and cloud scores

When Google sign-in and cloud-score synchronization are available and used, Google sign-in/Firebase Authentication may process account identifiers, email address and profile information made available by the sign-in provider. Math Rush may store a user-specific Firestore document containing a best score, display name and update timestamp. These services are used for account management and cloud score functionality. Guest-mode scores remain local and do not require an account.

Firebase and Google may process service-related technical information as described in their own documentation. Signing out stops the app from using the signed-in session but does not itself delete the account or associated cloud data.

Advertising, consent and technical data

Math Rush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request interstitial ads at configured game-session points. The SDK and related services may collect or receive technical information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The exact data processed depends on SDK versions, settings, consent state and use; consult Google's current disclosures linked below.

The app applies a maximum ad content rating of G and child-directed/under-age-of-consent treatment where configured for the all-ages experience. These settings do not guarantee that no technical data is processed or that every unsuitable ad category is blocked. The developer must configure AdMob blocking controls, consent messages and Google Play target-audience declarations appropriately. Ads are requested only when the app's consent flow indicates an ad request is permitted.

The Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK may refresh consent information, show messages configured by the publisher, and present privacy options where required. A consent or privacy-options form may not appear on every launch; availability depends on region, publisher configuration and consent state.

How information is used

  • Provide gameplay, local preferences and score features.
  • Authenticate users and synchronize scores to the cloud when those features are enabled and used.
  • Request and display interstitial ads, measure ad delivery and help prevent invalid activity.
  • Provide advertising consent and privacy choices where required.
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests.

Account and data deletion

Where account functionality is available, use the in-app Privacy & Account → Delete account option and confirm the action to request deletion of the Firebase Authentication account and its associated cloud user/score document. The app must successfully complete the relevant deletion operations for deletion to be complete. If an error appears, do not assume the account or cloud data has been deleted; contact Kabir201989@gmail.com for assistance.

Users who cannot access the app can use the Math Rush account-deletion page to find instructions and contact the developer. A request sent by email requires the developer to verify the request and complete deletion; opening an email draft does not itself delete data. Do not send passwords, verification codes, access tokens or ID tokens.

Deletion covers the Firebase Authentication account and the associated Firestore users/{uid} document used by Math Rush. It does not automatically erase local guest data on a device, third-party service logs, or records that a provider must retain for legitimate legal/security reasons. Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove local guest data.

Children and all-ages use

Math Rush has no age-selection screen and does not request a date of birth. The developer configures advertising treatment and ad content controls for the intended audience, but those technical settings do not by themselves determine the app's legal or Google Play classification. The publisher must make accurate target-audience and Families declarations and comply with applicable child privacy, consent and advertising requirements. Ad category blocking is an additional safeguard, not a guarantee that every unsuitable ad is blocked.

Retention and security

Local preferences and guest scores remain in app storage until cleared or the app is uninstalled. Account and cloud-profile data remains in Firebase until the associated account/document deletion succeeds, subject to technical propagation and applicable legal/security retention requirements. Google, Firebase and advertising providers may retain information under their own policies. No online service can be guaranteed completely risk-free.
