Math Rush – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Developer: TechStyleKabir

Contact: Kabir201989@gmail.com

1. About Math Rush

Math Rush is a timed arithmetic game designed for children, students and adults. It provides three difficulty levels: Junior, Classic and Pro. These are gameplay difficulty levels and are not age categories or age restrictions.

The game supports English and Hindi, timed rounds, multiple-choice questions, sound effects, background music and gameplay scores. Players can use guest mode. Google Sign-In and cloud score features are also available.

Math Rush does not ask players to select an age group or enter their date of birth.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Math Rush stores certain gameplay information locally, which may include your best score, language preference and gameplay settings. Guest-mode scores are stored on your device and do not require an account.

Local information generally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the application. The in-app account deletion process also clears the relevant local best-score preference.

Core gameplay does not require access to precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or personal files.

3. Google Sign-In and Cloud Score Synchronization

Google Sign-In is optional. If you choose to sign in, Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication may process information such as your account identifier, email address and profile information provided by Google.

Math Rush uses Cloud Firestore for account-related score functionality. The associated user document may contain your Firebase user ID, display name, best score and update timestamp. This information is used to provide account and cloud score features.

Guest gameplay does not require Google Sign-In. Guest scores remain stored locally unless you use an account-based feature.

Signing out does not automatically delete your Firebase account or associated cloud data.

4. Advertising and Advertising Identifiers

Math Rush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and display interstitial advertisements at configured game-session points. The app does not configure banner advertisements.

Depending on SDK version, device settings, consent status and advertising configuration, Google advertising services may collect or receive technical information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • App interactions and advertising interactions.
  • Device information and technical diagnostics.
  • Information used for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Math Rush is configured with child-directed and under-age-of-consent advertising treatment where applicable to its all-ages experience, and a maximum ad content rating of G. The developer also configures advertising category controls and other applicable safeguards.

These settings do not mean that no technical information is processed. They also do not guarantee that every unsuitable advertisement will be blocked.

Advertising requests are subject to the app's consent and advertising configuration. Ad availability can depend on consent, network connectivity, regional availability and advertising inventory. An advertisement is not guaranteed to appear after every round.

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Math Rush integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to support advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

Depending on your location, consent status and the publisher's configuration, the UMP SDK may display a consent message or provide access to privacy options. These messages may not appear on every app launch.

You can also review applicable privacy choices through the options made available within the application.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Math Rush and its service providers may be used to:

  • Provide gameplay and store local game preferences.
  • Provide optional account sign-in and cloud score synchronization.
  • Request and display advertisements.
  • Measure advertising delivery and prevent invalid advertising activity.
  • Support advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Respond to support and account deletion requests.

7. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Math Rush integrates services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms. Information may be transmitted to these providers to provide authentication, cloud score, advertising, consent and related services.

The handling of information by these services depends on their SDK versions, configuration, applicable consent requirements and the features you use.

8. Children and All-Ages Use

Math Rush is intended for an all-ages audience, including children and adults. The Junior, Classic and Pro modes are difficulty settings available to all players; they do not represent age groups.

The game does not provide an age-selection screen or request a date of birth. Advertising and data-processing safeguards are configured for the intended audience and applicable requirements.

The developer aims to maintain an appropriate experience for players of different ages. Advertising category controls are additional safeguards and cannot guarantee that every advertisement will be suitable for every individual.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you have used Google Sign-In, you can request deletion through the in-app Privacy & Account → Delete account option. The process requires the applicable account reauthentication and deletion operations to succeed.

A successful deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and its associated Math Rush Firestore user document. The in-app process also clears the relevant local best-score preference.

If deletion fails or an error appears, do not assume that the account or cloud data has been deleted. Contact the developer for assistance.

If you cannot access the application, you can find instructions on our Math Rush Account Deletion page. Submitting an email request does not automatically delete your account. The developer must verify and complete the request.

Account deletion does not automatically remove local guest information from your device, third-party provider logs or information that providers may need to retain for legitimate legal or security reasons. Clear app storage or uninstall the application to remove local guest data.

10. Data Retention and Security

Local gameplay preferences and guest scores remain on your device until cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Account and cloud score information is retained in Firebase until the applicable account and associated data deletion operations succeed, subject to technical processing and applicable legal or security retention requirements.

Third-party providers may retain certain information according to their own policies. The developer takes reasonable steps to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted or stored online.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose to play as a guest instead of signing in with Google. You may also clear local app data, uninstall the game, use available advertising privacy options and request deletion of an account where applicable.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, integrated services, data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, together with its effective date.
