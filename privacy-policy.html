About Math Rush

Math Rush is a timed arithmetic game with scores, streaks, hints, sound effects, English/Hindi interface options, and optional cloud score synchronisation. On first launch, the app asks the player to choose the age/audience path that accurately applies to them. The app does not request a date of birth. The child/younger-player path is guest-only and does not offer Google sign-in or cloud score synchronisation. The teen/adult path offers optional Google sign-in.

Information stored on the device

Math Rush stores the best score, language preference, and selected audience path in local app preferences. The app does not request Android permissions for precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Guest mode uses local storage and does not require the player to create an account. Local app preferences remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, except that the in-app account-deletion flow also clears the local best-score preference.

Google sign-in and cloud scores

If a player chooses the teen/adult path and signs in with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication. This can process the Firebase user ID, display name and email address returned by the identity provider, as well as technical request data such as IP address, user-agent strings, and the Firebase Android App ID, as described in Firebase's documentation. Cloud Firestore stores the Firebase user ID as the document key and stores the display name, best score and last-updated timestamp to synchronise the best score.

Google sign-in is optional. It is not offered in the child/younger-player path. Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore process information to provide sign-in and score synchronisation. Firebase's published disclosure states that its Android SDK data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and may be processed by service providers/subprocessors to provide Firebase services.

Advertising and consent

Math Rush uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for one interstitial-ad attempt at the end of a game session. The app does not intentionally display banner ads in this version. Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure lists data it automatically collects and shares for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation, including IP address, app/product interactions (such as launches, taps and ad interactions), diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. Review Google's current disclosure for additional detail.

The app applies a maximum ad content rating of G. In the child/younger-player path, it applies child-directed advertising treatment and the UMP under-age-of-consent signal, disables Google sign-in/cloud score sync, and limits ad content to G. These settings are not a promise that the advertising SDK processes no technical data. Ads are requested only when the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK indicates that an ad request is permitted.

UMP is used to refresh consent information on launch, display any consent messages configured by the publisher in AdMob Privacy & messaging, and show a privacy-options form when UMP says one is required. A consent form is not guaranteed to appear in every region or on every launch; this depends on published message configuration and the current consent state.

How information is used

  • To provide gameplay, store local preferences and display the player's best score.
  • For players who choose Google sign-in, to authenticate the account and synchronise the best score through Firebase Authentication and Firestore.
  • To request and show an interstitial at the natural end of a game session, measure ad delivery and help prevent invalid activity.
  • To provide advertising consent and privacy choices where required.

Account and data deletion

Signed-in users can open Privacy & Account in Math Rush and choose Delete account and cloud data. The app asks the user to re-authenticate the same Google account, deletes that account's Firestore user document, and then deletes the Firebase Authentication account. If re-authentication, network access, or deletion fails, the app reports that deletion did not complete; the user should retry or submit a request through the external deletion page.

Users who no longer have the app can use the Math Rush account-deletion page to request deletion. The published template opens an email draft and does not submit or fulfil a request automatically. The developer must monitor the listed support inbox, verify the request, and delete the matching Firebase Authentication account and Firestore user document. Do not include passwords or authentication tokens in deletion requests. Guest-mode scores and language/audience preferences remain local to the device and can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Children and audience selection

Math Rush provides a neutral first-launch audience choice intended to separate child/younger-player use from the optional Google/Firebase account features. The child/younger-player path uses guest gameplay, child-directed ad treatment, the UMP under-age-of-consent signal, and a G maximum ad content rating. The store's declared target audience must still accurately reflect how the app is designed, marketed and presented. Parents and users should review the third-party policies below for information about SDK processing. The app does not ask for a birth date.

Retention and security

Local best scores, language and audience preferences remain in app storage until removed by the app's clear/delete behaviour, app-data clearing or uninstall. Cloud account/profile data remains in Firebase until the account and associated Firestore document are deleted, subject to technical deletion propagation and any applicable legal/security retention requirements. Google/Firebase and AdMob may retain data under their own published policies. No internet service can be guaranteed completely risk-free; avoid sending passwords or sensitive information in support requests.
